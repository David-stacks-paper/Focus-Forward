Careers in Decline

Data Entry Clerks, Copywriters, and Content Writers

Routine, rule-bound, and repetitive tasks Companies increasingly use AI to draft high-volume marketing copy, basic product descriptions, social media posts, and formulaic blog content.

Telemarketers

AI-powered systems can automate customer calls and basic sales interactions. Voice AI can answer common questions, follow scripts, and contact customers, reducing the need for human telemarketers in some roles.

Cashiers and Ticket clerks

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) and the World Economic Forum Future of Jobs Report consistently rank cashiers and ticket clerks among the fastest-declining and most vulnerable clerical roles globally.
